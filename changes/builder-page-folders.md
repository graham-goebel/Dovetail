On the Builder page (`builder.html`), Pages can be dragged into a new order and kept in folders.

- Drag a page by the grip at its left to put it anywhere in the list. A line shows where it will land.
- New folder in the Pages header makes a folder, named right away. Drop a page on the folder, or between the pages in it, to put it there; a page's menu offers Move to each folder and Out of the folder too. A folder opens and closes, renames on a double-click, moves up and down with its pages, and is deleted without its pages, which stay where they were.
- A page added after one in a folder goes in the folder. Folders come along when a project is duplicated or downloaded as a file.
