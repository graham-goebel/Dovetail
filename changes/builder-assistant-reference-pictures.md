The Builder's assistant takes pictures with a message: attach up to three with the picture button, or paste or drop them into the message box. Each is scaled to fit 1568px and sent as a reference, and the assistant rebuilds its structure with the system's layouts and components, then compares what it made. Shared conversations keep only a note that a picture was sent.
