The Builder's assistant can try directions side by side. Its make_variants tool copies a frame once per direction, beside it, and builds each; a question's Try them all asks for the same. The reply's card lists the variants with Show, and Keep puts the chosen one in the original's place and name and removes the rest in one step, or Keep the original removes them all. Undo brings them back.
