The Builder's canvas draws a Carousel as it will look, its layout (coverflow, ring, marquee and the rest) held still at its first item, instead of a flat row. Selecting the carousel or one of its items lays it flat so items can be picked, reordered and dropped in, as before; it moves in Play.
