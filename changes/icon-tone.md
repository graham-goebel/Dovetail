`Icon` takes a `tone`: one of the text roles (`primary`, `secondary`, `tertiary`, `headline`, `link`, `brand`, `brand-secondary`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`), read from `--dt-text-*`. The default, `inherit`, keeps taking the colour of the text around it.
