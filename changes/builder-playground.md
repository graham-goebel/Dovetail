The Builder (`builder.html`) has a **Playground** project on Home, with getting-started pages and live examples to take apart.
- **Start here** walks through the builder in seven pages: welcome, styling a card, laying out a row, freeform and structured frames, light, dark and themes, using the components in Preview and Play, and the keys worth knowing.
- **Examples**: a landing page with its phone version, a store, a settings form, a support chat, social posts, and the builder's own screens drawn in a freeform frame.
- Each browser gets the Playground once. A first visit opens Start here. Deleting it keeps it deleted, and **New → Playground** on Home adds a fresh copy.
