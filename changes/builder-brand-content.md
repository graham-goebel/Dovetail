The Builder page (`builder.html`) has a Brand card in Content, holding the project's name, logo and brand mark.

- Name the brand, and upload or replace the logo and brand mark, right in Content. These are the same as Configure's Brand group, and each project keeps its own. A picture over 512KB is turned away with a message.
- Drag the logo or mark onto a frame, or press it to add it. Each goes in as an uncropped picture (`fit: contain`, no rounding), named Logo or Brand mark. Without a logo file, the logo is the name, set as a Heading in the wordmark's colour.
- A brand set in Configure is now saved with the project too. Before, only colour, type and the other settings were. Switching projects also puts the project's own brand back in the header.
