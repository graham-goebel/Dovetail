The Builder has a Context panel in its left rail for what the design assistant reads besides the canvas. A doc is markdown, written in the panel or uploaded. A skill is a SKILL.md and the files beside it, uploaded as a folder or a zip, or written in the panel. Each is kept for this file, its project, or the team. A doc is used always, only when attached, or not at all, and a file's doc can be limited to some of its pages. A skill can be switched off, and its SKILL.md front matter sets its name and when it's used. A meter shows what the current page would send, in docs, skills and rough tokens. Everything is kept in this browser for now.
