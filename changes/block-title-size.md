`HeroBlock`, `CtaBlock`, `FeatureGridBlock`, `SplitBlock`, `StatsBlock`, `FaqBlock`, `TestimonialBlock` and `ProductGridBlock` take `titleSize`, which sets the title's size on the heading and display scale (`display-lg`, `display-md`, `display-sm`, `heading-xl`, `heading-lg`, `heading-md`). `HeroBlock` defaults to `display-sm` and the others to `heading-lg`, as before. The heading level stays the same.
