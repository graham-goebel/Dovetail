The Builder's Fill section has a *Gradient* menu of the system's own gradients and textures: `--dt-surface-brand-gradient`, `--dt-surface-brand-duotone`, the three scrims (`--dt-scrim-bottom`, `--dt-scrim-top`, `--dt-scrim-fade-bottom`) and the two patterns (`--dt-pattern-dots`, `--dt-pattern-grid`). Each follows the theme and dark mode, and wins over a custom fill colour. The Layer section has *Blur* (`--dt-blur-chip` or `--dt-blur-glass` on the layer itself) and *Behind* (`--dt-backdrop-glass`, blurring what shows through it). A layer that's inverted and blurred keeps both in one `filter`.
