On the Builder page (`builder.html`), the left panels are tidier and easier to search.
- **Pages:** the drag grips are gone. Drag a page's row to move it, or on a touch screen, hold the row a moment first so a swipe still scrolls. A drag doesn't also open the page.
- **Search on every panel:** Pages and Configure get the same floating search at the foot of the panel as Assets, Layers and Content. Pages filters by page or folder name. Configure narrows its groups by name and summary, and a group's settings by their words.
- **Content:** photos and clips fill their tiles, two across at 4:3, so they're easier to tell apart. Illustrations and cut-outs stay whole on the checkerboard. The note saying whose content it is lines up with the panel's title.
- **Assets:** the first level shows an icon for each kind, with its name and count. Primitives uses the blend modes' swatch icon. Live previews stay one level down.
- **Component previews:** a preview now waits for the system to load, and refits whenever its tile changes size, so a preview that was measured too early no longer stays blank or mis-scaled.
