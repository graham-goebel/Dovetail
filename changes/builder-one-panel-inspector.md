The Builder's inspector is now one scrolling panel instead of Properties, Appearance and Layout tabs. It runs top to bottom: a layer's content and style, its auto layout, then Position, Size, Layer, Fill, Border, Corners and Shadow, with Spacing last. Spacing starts folded unless something in it is set. A Border, Corners or Shadow that isn't set is just its title and an add button. A frame's panel is its kind, its look, then its layout. Switching a frame to Structured scrolls to the auto layout, and a spacing label on the canvas brings its section into view. The pin square, the align matrix and the box model are unchanged.
