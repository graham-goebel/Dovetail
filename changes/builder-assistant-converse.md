The Builder's assistant asks before big changes and keeps its conversation. Before a new page or frame, or a change that adds more than about 10 layers, it shows a plan (the frame, the steps, anything to know) and waits for Build it or Change plan; such changes are refused until a plan is approved. A note typed while it works lands with its next step. Each change on the card can be undone back to that point, and Undo all still takes the whole reply. The ⋯ menu turns plans and looking at the canvas on or off and exports or clears the thread; Quick or Careful sets how hard it thinks. Each file keeps its conversation in this browser.
