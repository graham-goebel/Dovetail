- Text and icon colour sit at the top of Content.
- Shift+A wraps any selection in an auto layout Group, and dragging one of several selected layers on the canvas moves them all.

- Pages and folders take an icon from the library or an emoji.
- The Agent can run on Opus, Sonnet or Haiku.
- Turn into is gone from the inspector's menus, and Stretch and Space between are icon toggles.

- On a tablet the panels fit what the browser actually shows.
