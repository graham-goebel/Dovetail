Configure's Color group has Page (White or Brand muted) and Sections (Grey or Brand muted), so a whole site, or just its quiet bands, can take the tint of the primary instead of white and grey. Both are written to the exported CSS and declared under `.dark`, so dark bands resolve the dark tint.
