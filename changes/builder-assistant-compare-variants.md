The Builder's assistant compares the variants it builds with a new `compare_frames` tool: a picture of each, its checks, and how alike each pair is section by section, with pairs that differ only in copy or a colour flagged as too alike. It grades each variant, rebuilds the weakest and gives the grades in its reply.
