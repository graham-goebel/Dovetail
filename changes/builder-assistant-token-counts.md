The Builder's assistant shows how many tokens each reply used, and the conversation's total at the foot of the thread. While a reply works, its count starts from an estimate of what's being sent. Once it's done, it shows the counts the model reported, with how much came from the prompt cache on hover. Practice mode, where nothing is sent, estimates at about four characters a token and marks its counts with ≈.
