Builder:
- A component on the canvas holds what's in it. The first click picks the component; once it's selected, a click picks what's inside it: a layer in one of its slots, or one of its own parts (its title, lead, a list item, its picture, its icon). Cmd or Ctrl clicks straight through.
- A part picked shows the props it comes from in the inspector: its words, the size or link that goes with it, or the picture. A list item edits just that item. The part is outlined on the canvas and named in the component's tag, and the part under the pointer is outlined too.
- Every part a component lists in Layers can be picked, not just its title.
- Escape goes from a part back to its component.
