`Badge` has two new tones, `brand` and `brand-secondary`, for marks that belong to the brand rather than a status, such as New or Members. With `variant="subtle"` the badge is the page's own surface (`--dt-surface-base`) with `--dt-text-brand` and `--dt-border-brand`, so it reads on the page and on a brand fill alike. With `variant="solid"` it is the brand fill with `--dt-text-on-brand`.
