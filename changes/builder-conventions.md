In the Builder (`builder.html`), shortcuts and a few everyday gestures now work the way other design tools have them.
- Shortcuts read as this keyboard says them: ⇧⌘G on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+G elsewhere, in menus, tooltips and notes.
- Press ? (or choose Keyboard shortcuts from the File menu) for a sheet of every shortcut, in groups.
- Any layer can be renamed, not only Groups: F2, a double-click on its row, or Rename in its menu. A name a layer is given, such as the brand Logo, now outlasts a reload.
- On a freeform canvas, Alt on a handle resizes from the centre, and Shift pressed once a move has begun keeps it to one axis.
- A picture on the clipboard (a screenshot, an image copied from a page) pastes as an Image and joins Content's images.
- Holding Alt measures the spacing, as Shift does.
- Export takes a picture at 1x, 2x or 3x, of the one selected layer when the Code shows one; Export as PNG is in a layer's right-click menu too.
- Fixes an edit made right after a change on the canvas (a key pressed just after a drag, for one) being made on the document as it was before that change, which undid the change.
