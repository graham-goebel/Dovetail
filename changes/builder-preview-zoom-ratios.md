In the Builder (`builder.html`), Play zooms, and a social frame takes a ratio.
- **Zoom in Play**: step in and out (the − and + buttons, or the - and + keys), Fit to the screen (Shift+1), fit its Width (Shift+2), or 100%, its actual size (Shift+0). Larger than the stage, the screen scrolls. Play still opens no larger than the screen's own size, small enough to fit.
- **Ratio on social frames**: a frame with social type has Square, 4:3 and 16:9 on its Layout tab. Each sets the height from the width (1080 × 1080, 1080 × 810, 1080 × 608).
