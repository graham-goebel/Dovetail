The Builder's assistant can look at a frame at another width or in the other mode. Its screenshot tool takes a width (390 for a phone) and dark on or off, and draws the frame that way out of sight, so the canvas stays as it is; each picture shows in the thread with what it looked at. Its brief asks it to look at 390 wide and in dark mode after changes to layout or colour.
