Accordion's disclosure icon is a plus that turns into a minus as a panel opens, in place of the chevron. Panels ease open and shut, with their height and opacity following `--dt-motion-emphasis`, so they respect reduced motion. A closed panel stays in the document but is hidden from the tab order and assistive technology.
