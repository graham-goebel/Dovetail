The builder gives every unnamed layer a name read from what it holds or what it is, so Layers, the checks and the assistant say "Rent · $2,400", "Rent, Food, Travel", "$6,214 grid", "Chips", "Bar chart" or "Glow" rather than "Group" or "Shape". A plain wrapper round one container is called that container's wrapper, so the two don't share a name. The name follows the content until you rename the layer, and clearing a rename brings it back.
