The builder layouts guide (`assets/builder-layouts.md`) lists what each component starts with when a layout leaves a prop out, worked out from the same specimens the builder reads, and says how to clear one with `""`. It documents Grid's `minColumnWidth`, notes that a scrolling page wants `mode: "structured"` and `hug: true`, explains the style options that read differently from their names (`w: fill`, `position: floating`, gradient over surface, layer blur against backdrop blur), and adds recipes for composing from Group and Shape: reflowing columns, tiles, glows, rings, bar charts, chips, big numbers and lines with ends.
