The Builder's assistant asks when the choice is yours, and knows what you changed. When a request leaves a real choice of direction open, its reply shows a question with two to four options, each naming what it would use; click one, or type something else, and it carries on. Your own canvas edits since its last reply show above the message box and go with your next message, so it builds on them; the × leaves them out.
