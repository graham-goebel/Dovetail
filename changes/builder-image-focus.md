The Builder offers an Image's `position` as *Focal point*, a menu of the nine CSS position keywords (center, top, bottom, left, right and the four corners), each with a picture of where the picture keeps its focus when its frame crops it. Center is the default and isn't written; any other exports as `position="top right"` and the like. Typed lengths stay out of the inspector, as before.
