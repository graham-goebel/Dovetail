In Configure, Color is its own group, apart from Brand. Brand holds the name and two uploads, a wordmark (shown in the header in place of the name) and a brand mark (a symbol beside it), each SVG or PNG up to 512KB, with the wordmark colour and an original or matched file colour. Space is now Layout, and its Whitespace control is Spacing, with Tight, Balanced and Open. The colour contrast pairs are collapsed by default; the summary still says how many fail.

Also on the docs site: the sidebar no longer has a filter, the page actions sheet drops its "This page" line, and the Space foundation page is Layout.
