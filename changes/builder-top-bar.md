The Builder page (`builder.html`) has a quieter top bar, and new frames come from Assets.

- The bar keeps the projects button, the project's name in the middle, zoom, Play, Preview and Export. Gone from it: the + button, Undo and Redo (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z, or Cmd on a Mac), the Saved mark, the panels toggle (Tab) and the copy-link button. The bar speaks up only when this browser can't keep your work.
- With something selected, the middle of the bar shows the path to it: project › frame › … › layer. Each step selects; the project's name still renames on a double-click. The inspector no longer repeats the path.
- Copy link lives in Export and in the project's menu (…), which also holds Paste a layout… and Start over with a blank frame.
- Assets opens on Containers: a freeform, structured or tall frame, and every screen size, from Phone to Story. Press one to add it beside your frames, or drag it onto the canvas to put it where you drop it. Templates stay their own kind.
- The canvas dots are quieter, and Layers has an icon of its own, so it no longer shares one with Blocks.
