The Builder page (`builder.html`) records every edit as small changes, each naming one thing on one frame, ready for sharing a canvas later.

- Undo and redo take back or remake your own steps on the canvas as it is now, rather than swapping in an earlier copy of the whole canvas. A change that arrived from elsewhere in the meantime stays.
- Undoing a step on another frame changes that frame without switching to it.
- Typing in place on the canvas is one step when you press Enter, and Escape takes it back without leaving a step.
