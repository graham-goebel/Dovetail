The Builder's inspector is quieter. Every menu and number field is one short filled field. Labels are small and muted, numbers use even figures, and section titles are small but heavier. A section you haven't set has a dimmed title. A field's hint is now a tooltip on its label instead of a line under the control, and screen readers still hear it. A layer's description and its docs link sit behind an info button by its name. The align, distribute and flip buttons in the inspector's header are borderless. The pin square, the align matrix and the box model keep their size.
