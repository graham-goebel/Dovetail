In the Builder (`builder.html`), each floating panel resizes by its inner edge, in steps of 4: the left panel from 280 to 520 wide, the inspector from 280 to 480. A readout shows the width as you drag, and the tool bar stays centred between them.
- Drag past the minimum and the panel folds away: the left one to its icon rail (any rail tab opens it again), the inspector out of sight (a button at the edge shows it). Either opens back at the width it had.
- Double-click an edge to put its default back. The edges take focus: Left and Right move them 4 (Shift 16), Home and End go to the limits, Enter folds or opens.
- Widths and folds are kept in this browser. On a phone the panels stay full-width panes.
