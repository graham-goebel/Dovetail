The Builder (`builder.html`) gains two internal pieces for an upcoming project export, with nothing on screen yet: a zip writer (`assets/builder/model/zip.js`) and `themeCss(theme)` (`assets/builder/model/theme.js`), which turns a project's saved theme into the same `theme.css` Configure's download gives, through the new `DovetailConfigurePanel.themeCss` in `assets/theme.js`.
